What is Color Blindness?

Color blindness affects approximately 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women worldwide. It changes how colors are perceived.

Why Simulate?

Understanding how others see colors helps create more accessible designs and empathize with different visual experiences.

Types Covered

We simulate all major types: Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia, Achromatopsia, and partial variants.
