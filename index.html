About Vision Deficiencies
Protanopia: Lack of red cones. Reds look dimmer and shift toward brown/green.
Deuteranopia: Lack of green cones. Difficulty distinguishing green and red.
Tritanopia: Lack of blue cones. Difficulty distinguishing blue and yellow.
